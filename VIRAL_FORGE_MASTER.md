## 💡 PRO TIPS FROM THE TRENCHES

### 🎯 Viral Secrets
1. **Pattern interrupt every 5 seconds** - Zoom, text, cut
2. **Open loops** - "Wait until you see #1..."
3. **Relatability** - "If you're like me..."
4. **Numbers** - Specific beats vague ($12,453 vs. "a lot")
5. **Controversy** - Hot takes get engagement

### 💰 Monetization Hacks
1. **Bundle affiliate links** - "My AI toolkit" > individual links
2. **Lead magnet first** - Email > Direct sale
3. **Free trials** - Lower barrier = more clicks
4. **Urgency** - "Limited time" works
5. **Social proof** - "10K people using this"

### 📱 Platform-Specific
**TikTok**:
- Captions ON by default (90% watch muted)
- Hashtags matter (use 5-10)
- Reply to comments with videos
- Duet/stitch trending content

**YouTube Shorts**:
- Keywords in description matter
- Link to long-form videos
- Playlist organization helps
- Shorts shelf = discovery gold

## 🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING

### "I'm not getting views"
**Fix**:
- Check hook strength (first 2 seconds)
- Test different posting times
- Use trending sounds/topics
- Engage with 10 creators daily
- Check video quality (lighting, audio)

### "No one clicks my links"
**Fix**:
- Stronger CTA ("Free guide in bio" vs. "Link in bio")
- Offer value first (lead magnet)
- Test different offers
- Make bio link obvious
- Add urgency/scarcity

### "I'm running out of ideas"
**Fix**:
- Follow the 30-day plan exactly
- Repurpose top performers (new angle)
- Check competitor content for inspiration
- Ask audience what they want (polls)
- Use ChatGPT for brainstorming
