## 🎨 Core Visual Identity

The Emberquill Wyrm is a majestic emerald dragon rendered in high-resolution pixel art, serving as the eternal guardian of creative magic and enchanted storytelling.

**Design Characteristics:**
- **Art Style:** 16-bit pixel art with modern detail enhancement
- **Pose:** Side-profile stance, wings fully unfurled
- **Scale:** Massive, commanding presence
- **Expression:** Proud, protective, wise

### Dragon Anatomy & Symbolism

**Scales - Emerald & Turquoise:**
- Deep green to turquoise gradient
- Shimmer like enchanted runes
- Symbolizes: Ancient magic, growth, renewal

**Chest Plating - Golden-Orange:**
- Molten metal appearance
- Runs along belly and underbelly
- Symbolizes: Wealth, prosperity, powerful storytelling

**Wings - Flame-Lit:**
- Bright orange and golden-yellow
- Vast, spreading across sky
- Symbolizes: Creativity ignited, expansion, brand elevation

**Horns - Fiery Orange:**
- Curved forward
- Glowing with inner fire
- Symbolizes: Wisdom, strength, bold spirit

**Body - Stone-Segmented:**
- Blocky, structured appearance
- Ancient infrastructure aesthetic
- Symbolizes: Reliability, unbreakable foundation

## 🎮 Visual Style Guide

### Pixel Art Aesthetic

**Grid System:**
- Base unit: 8px (classic pixel grid)
- Sprites: Multiples of 8px (16px, 24px, 32px, 64px)
- Icons: 32x32px or 64x64px

**Pixel Rendering:**
- Sharp edges, no anti-aliasing on sprites
- CSS: `image-rendering: pixelated`
- Crisp, defined outlines

**Color Application:**
- Bold, saturated colors
- Strong contrast for readability
- Limited palette per element (3-5 colors max)

### Typography

**Headings:**
- Font: Press Start 2P (pixel font) or Archivo Black (bold medieval)
- Transform: Uppercase for major headings
- Letter-spacing: Slightly increased for pixelated feel

**Body Text:**
- Font: DM Sans (clean, readable)
- Keep modern and legible
- Pixel fonts only for decorative elements

**Accent Text:**
- Font: Fraunces (medieval serif)
- Use for quest names, special callouts

**Rune Effects:**
- Small rotating pixels around elements
- Emerald/turquoise color shifts
- Symbolize: Active magic, processing

**Sky Backgrounds:**
- Layered pixel-art clouds
- Blue gradient (light to deeper blue)

## 🌟 Animation Guidelines

### Dragon Animations

**Wing Flap:**
- Duration: 2-3 seconds
- Easing: ease-in-out
- Loop: Infinite or on hover

**Scale Shimmer:**
- Subtle color shift (emerald ↔ turquoise)
- Duration: 4-6 seconds
- Opacity: 0.8-1.0

**Horn Glow:**
- Pulsating orange glow
- Duration: 2 seconds
- Use: Active processing states

### Pixel Effects

**Pixel Sparkle:**
- Small 4px x 4px blocks
- Random appearance/disappearance
- Colors: Emerald, turquoise, gold

**Rune Rotation:**
- 8px x 8px pixel symbols
- 360° rotation
- Speed: 3-4 seconds

---

## 📐 Layout Principles

### Grid & Spacing

**8px Base Grid:**
- All spacing in multiples of 8
- 16px: Tight spacing
- 24px: Standard spacing
- 32px: Generous spacing
- 64px: Section breaks

### Pixel Borders

**Standard Border:**
- 4px solid #1A1A2E
- Box shadow: 8px 8px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.2)
- Creates pixelated 3D effect

### Card Layouts

**Quest Cards:**
- Border: 4px solid black
- Background: Cloud White
- Shadow: Offset pixel shadow
- Hover: Lift effect (transform: translateY(-4px))

## 🛡️ Brand Protection

### Do's

✅ Use dragon imagery respectfully and powerfully
✅ Maintain pixel-art aesthetic consistency
✅ Keep colors vibrant and saturated
✅ Apply medieval metaphors tastefully
✅ Balance fantasy with professionalism

### Don'ts

❌ Don't use low-quality or blurry dragon art
❌ Don't mix pixel art with realistic renders
❌ Don't oversaturate with too many effects
❌ Don't make text unreadable with heavy theming
❌ Don't abandon modern UX for pure aesthetics
